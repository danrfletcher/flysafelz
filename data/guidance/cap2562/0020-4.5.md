Prosecution
Breaches of aviation regulations are normally a criminal offence. More serious cases of flying that endangers people on the ground or other aircraft may attract a fine or prison sentence.

Endangering an aircraft carries a maximum sentence of five years in prison and endangering persons or property carries a maximum of two years.
