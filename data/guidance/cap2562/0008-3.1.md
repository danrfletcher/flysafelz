Navigation
Before venturing away from the launch site, you must carry an appropriate aviation chart and know how to navigate, otherwise you may become lost and infringe controlled airspace or other restrictions.

Using a ‘moving map’ GNSS device with aeronautical charting will enhance your situational awareness.
