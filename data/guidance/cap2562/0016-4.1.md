ENSURE THE MASTER SWITCH IS OFF
You have a responsibility to maintain your paramotor in a safe and airworthy condition. Follow the maintenance schedules provided by the manufacturer and inspect your aircraft before the start of each flying day.

Allow adequate time and conduct your checks in a suitable location. If you are unsure about how to perform the set up and checks correctly, consult an appropriately qualified instructor.
