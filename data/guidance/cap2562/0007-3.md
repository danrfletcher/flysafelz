Airspace
Be aware of your altitude - do not climb into controlled airspace You must understand the airspace environment you are flying in Controlled Airspace > Airspace is defined either as uncontrolled airspace or controlled airspace.

> Controlled airspace is located around airports but also at higher levels.

Terminal airspace and enroute airways are found throughout the UK at various altitudes.

For operations inside controlled airspace, even at low altitude, you must have the permission of the relevant air traffic control unit.

Unauthorised entry into controlled airspace causes a significant risk – paramotors may not be visible to air traffic control on radar and you may collide with other aircraft.

Full details of airspace classifications may be found in the Skyway Code .
