Airspace hazards
Understand key airspace hazards and restrictions.

For more information read the Skyway Code .

Many aerodromes are surrounded by an Air Traffic Zone (ATZ):

Make contact with the airfield over the radio and obtain either information (uncontrolled airfields) or permission (controlled airfields); or > If the aerodrome permits non- radio traffic, obtain permission in advance of the flight.

> For further details, consult the Airspace section of the Skyway Code .
