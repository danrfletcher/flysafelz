MODES AND CODES
> Squawk 7000 (VFR) or 2000 (IFR) as the conspicuity code; > Select mode ‘C’ at all times so that your altitude is displayed; and > If ‘listening in’ on a frequency, use a relevant frequency monitoring code ( see p.1 2) .

Non-normal situations:

> 7700

– Emergency code. Should be selected as soon as practical if the aircraft is suffering an emergency.

> 7600

– Radio failure.

> 7500

– Unlawful interference.

> 0030

– Lost.

More details on transponder procedures can be found at Section 2 of ENR 1 .6 of the AIP .
