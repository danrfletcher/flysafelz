The only exceptions are if in compliance with ATC direction or local procedure, or when in airspace not requiring transponder carriage, turning off the transponder is permitted if necessary to conserve electrical power.

Turning off a serviceable transponder and will cause increased risk to other airspace users.

Doing so in the event of infringing controlled airspace will also increase the likely sanction.
