PART-FCL VS ANO LICENCES
Part-FCL pilot licences are issued under the UK Aircrew Regulation and are required to fly Part 21 aeroplanes and helicopters. Part- FCL licences, issued while the UK was still a member of EASA, continue to be valid on UK-registered Part 21 aircraft. A Part-FCL licence may also be used to fly a non-Part 21 aircraft of an equivalent aircraft category and class. To fly a non-Part 21 aircraft requiring a type rating, you need a UK licence issued under the ANO.

Licences issued under the ANO may only be used to fly non-Part 21 aircraft, with the exception that you may use an ANO licence to fly a Part 21 aircraft under Part-NCO and within the privileges of the following ratings:

Single Engine Piston (SEP), Simple Single Engine Aeroplane (SSEA), Self-Launching Motor Glider (SLMG) or Touring Motor Glider (TMG).
