AIRFRAME ICING
If you enter visible moisture when the outside air temperature is 0 oC or less, ice may form on the aircraft. Ice has a very detrimental effect on aircraft performance, so must be avoided, unless the aircraft is approved for flight in icing conditions. The worst airframe icing will normally occur between 0 oC and –15 oC.

Always remove any frost, ice or snow from the aircraft before flight. Ensure the pitot probe and static ports are clear of contamination.
