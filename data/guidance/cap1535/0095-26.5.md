TRANSPONDER MANDATORY ZONE
> A TMZ is an area established within which all aircraft must be equipped with the type of transponder specified for a particular zone, and operate in accordance with any required instructions.

> UK TMZs normally require a mode S transponder ( see p.

89) . Aircraft not equipped with mode Smust request the permission of the relevant ATSU before entering.

> Details of individual TMZs can be found in GEN 1 .5 and ENR 2.2 of the AIP .
