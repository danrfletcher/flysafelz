UK AIRCREW REGULATION
Requirements for the licensing of aircrew are contained in UK Regulation (EU) 1178/2011 , known as the UK Aircrew Regulation. The Regulation contains several annexes, including Part-FCL and Part- MED. Part-FCL contains the requirements and privileges for flight crew licences and ratings. A licence issued in accordance with UK Part-FCL is known as a Part-FCL licence. Part-MED contains medical certification requirements.
