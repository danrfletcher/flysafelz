Incident and accident reporting
If you experience a safety incident, you may be legally obliged to report it. In all cases the information shared with the relevant organisations will be treated as confidential (not anonymous). Accidents, serious incidents and Airproxes are subject to public reports, but without identifying the persons involved. All investigations into aviation safety events will be conducted in accordance with ‘Just Culture’ principles.
