UK AIR OPERATIONS REGULATION
Operational requirements for Part 21 aircraft are contained in UK Regulation (EU) 965/2012 , known as the UK Air Operations Regulation.

General aviation pilots are primarily concerned with the Part-NCO annex of the Air Operations Regulation. Part-NCO contains pilot responsibilities, operational rules and aircraft equipment requirements. It is applicable to non-commercial and flight training operations with ‘other-than- complex motor-powered’ Part 21 aeroplanes and helicopters.
