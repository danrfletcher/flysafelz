PART 21 VS NON-PART 21 AIRCRAFT
Part 21 aircraft would have been ‘EASA aircraft’ while the UK was still a member of the EU. Most modern factory built aircraft (other than microlights and gyroplanes) are considered Part 21 .

Non-Part 21 aircraft are regulated by the UK Air Navigation Order (ANO) 2016. Vintage and ex- military aircraft, amateur built aircraft, microlights, gyroplanes are normally non-Part 21 . Such aircraft will have a certificate of airworthiness or permit to fly issued under the ANO 2016, unless exempt from airworthiness certification.

Foot-launched aircraft such as hang gliders, paragliders and self-propelled hang or paragliders are also non-Part 21 . A full description of the non-Part 21 criteria can be found in the CAA GA webpages .
