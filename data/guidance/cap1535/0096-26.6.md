RADIO MANDATORY ZONE
> An RMZ is an area established within which all aircraft must establish two- way communication over or monitor (as determined for the individual RMZ) the relevant radio communication frequency. Use the freecall ( see p.

80) format to establish contact.

> Details of individual RMZs can be found in GEN 1 .5 and ENR 2.2 of the AIP .
