Frequency Monitoring Codes
UK COMMS INFO Frequency Monitoring Codes (FMC) should be selected on your aircraft’s transponder while monitoring the applicable ATC frequency, without having established radio communication.

Remember to return your transponder to the appropriate conspicuity code (VFR – 7000, IFR – 2000) when no longer monitoring the relevant frequency.
