RULES OF THE AIR 2015
The UK Rules of the Air Regulation 2015 is made under the ANO. The Regulation contains a small number of rules that are additional to those in SERA. It applies to all aircraft in UK airspace.

The requirements relevant to GA are highlighted throughout the Skyway Code .
