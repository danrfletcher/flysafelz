Move horizontally right (from pilot's point of view).

(20) Cross arms with wands downwards and in front of body.

Land.

(21) Move right-hand wand in a "fanning" motion from shoulder to knee, while at the same time pointing with left-hand wand to area of fire.

Fire.

(22) Fully extend arms and wands downwards at a 45- degree angle to sides. Hold position until aircraft is clear for next manoeuvre.

Hold position/stand by.

(25) Hold arms fully extended above head, open left hand horizontally and move finger tips of right hand into a touch open palm of left hand (forming a "T"). At night, illuminated wands can also be used to form the "T" above head.
