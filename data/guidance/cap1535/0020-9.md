Assimilated law
KEY REGULATIONS 2 SERA and the UK Rules of the Air 2015 also apply to UK registered aircraft outside UK airspace, but only to the extent that compliance does not conflict with the rules of the state in which the aircraft is flying The Skyway Code / Key Regulations
