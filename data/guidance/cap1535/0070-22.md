Flight plans
PRE-FLIGHT PREPARATION !

Key info A flight plan is the transmission of information to air traffic service units (ATSU) regarding the intended route of a particular flight or portion of a flight.

Flight plans are either:

> Full flight plans, which are filed through the Aeronautical Fixed Telecommunication Network (AFTN); or > Abbreviated flight plans, which may be passed by radio or telephone.
