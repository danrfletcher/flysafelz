UK CONTINUING AIRWORTHINESS REGULATION
Continuing airworthiness and maintenance requirements for Part 21 aircraft are contained in UK Regulation (EU) 1321/2014 , known as the UK Continuing Airworthiness Regulation.

Most owners or operators of Part 21 light aircraft will be concerned with the Part-ML annex of the regulation. More information about Part-ML is available in the Airworthiness Code and the Part-ML pages of the CAA website.
