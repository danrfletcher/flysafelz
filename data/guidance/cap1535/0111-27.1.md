WHAT SERVICE TO ASK FOR?
> GA pilots operating under VFR should request either a ‘Basic’ or ‘Traffic’ service.

> In good weather conditions there is potentially a greater likelihood of mid-air collision, due to increased traffic density. Consider requesting a ‘Traffic Service’ for increased situational awareness.
