Flight Information Regions
London and Scottish Information can provide a ‘ Basic Service ’ to all civil aircraft in UK airspace. Subject to capacity, other services such as opening flight plans or weather information are also available. Note London/Scottish Information are not equipped with surveillance (radar). Any transponder code assigned is for the awareness of other ATC units. For more details of UK Flight Information Services, see p.
