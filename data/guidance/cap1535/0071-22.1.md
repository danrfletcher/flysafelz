FULL FLIGHT PLAN
Details of how to file a full VFR flight plan using the AFPEx system are contained in the ‘International Flying’ chapter ( see p.1 62) .

Most flight planning applications also offer integrated flight plan filing and guidance on AFTN addressing. A full flight plan may be filed for any flight, but it is a requirement for flying internationally. It is also recommended to file one if:

> Flying over water, more than 10 NM from the UK coastline; > When flying to the Scottish Highlands and Islands aerodromes; and > Over other sparsely populated areas where search and rescue might be difficult.
