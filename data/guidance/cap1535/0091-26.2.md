AERODROMES WITHOUT ATZ
Some aerodromes without an ATZ are denoted on the chart with an outer circle around the aerodrome symbol and a letter ‘T’ or ‘U’.

The ‘T’ indicates intensive use for training flights. The ‘U’ means unusual activity such as aerobatics or formation flying takes place.

It is preferable to avoid overflight of these aerodromes. If passing nearby, consider making contact on the applicable radio frequency.
