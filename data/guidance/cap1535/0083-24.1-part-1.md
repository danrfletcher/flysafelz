SERA VMC MINIMA
*Class Dexception:

Under the following circumstances you may fly ‘clear of cloud’ in class Dairspace, without complying with the specific cloud separation distances: by day at or below 3,000 ft AMSL, in sight of the surface and at 140 kts IAS or less. 5 km visibility is still required, except for helicopters for which 1,500 m is required. Note this is a UK difference from Part-SERA as applies in the EU.

*Class Gexception:

In UK class Gairspace the 5 km requirement may be reduced to 1,500 m, if flying by day, in sight of the surface and at 140 kts IAS or less.

Under the following circumstances you may fly ‘clear of cloud’ in class Dairspace, without complying with the specific cloud separation distances: by day at or below 3,000 ft AMSL, in sight of the surface and at 140 kts IAS or less. 5 km visibility is still required, except for helicopters for which 1,500 m is required.
