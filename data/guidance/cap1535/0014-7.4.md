SAILPLANE AND BALLOON REGULATIONS
Operational and licensing rules for Part 21 sailplanes and balloons became applicable in 2019. These regulations continue in the UK as assimilated law:

> Balloons – UK Regulation (EU) 2018/395 > Sailplanes – UK Regulation (EU) 2018/1976 The requirement to hold a pilot’s licence in accordance with the assimilated law is currently deferred until 30th September 2025. Before this date, it is acceptable to fly a Part 21 balloon under the privileges of an ANO licence, or a Part 21 sailplane with the appropriate British Gliding Association (BGA) certificate.

Non-Part 21 gliders and balloons will continue to be regulated by the ANO 2016.
