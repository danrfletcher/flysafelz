I AM SAFE
The following mnemonic is recommended for assessing fitness to fly:

Illness Do I have any symptoms that might affect my ability to fly?

Attitude Am I emotionally ready and fully focussed on the flight?

Medication Am I taking any prescription or over- the-counter drugs that might affect my performance?

Stress Am I under pressure or have any worries and anxieties?

Alcohol Have I been drinking within the last 24 hours?

Fatigue Am I tired or not adequately rested?

Eating Am I adequately nourished?
