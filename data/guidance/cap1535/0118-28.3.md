FREQUENCY MONITORING CODES
Frequency Monitoring Codes (previously Listening Squawks) are a situational awareness tool that benefit both pilots and air traffic controllers. They allow the identification of aircraft that are monitoring a particular radio frequency. Using an FMC may mitigate the risk of airspace infringement.

> Listen out on the applicable frequency; > Set your transponder to the corresponding FMC; and > Remember to squawk the appropriate conspicuity code when no longer monitoring the frequency.
