ABBREVIATED FLIGHT PLAN
An abbreviated flight plan is essentially the passing of information to an ATSU for a particular portion of your flight either by telephone or over the radio. For example this might be on departure from an aerodrome or when requesting a clearance to cross controlled airspace.

Unlike a full flight plan, an abbreviated flight plan will not be transmitted to any other ATSU.
