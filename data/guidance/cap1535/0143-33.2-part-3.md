Larger aerodromes may also have ‘guard lights’ – normally these are placed in pairs either side of a runway holding point and alternately flash yellow.

The Skyway Code / Aerodrome Operations Area unfit for the movement of aircraft Aerodrome boundary Boundary markers Orange and white markers are sometimes used to mark the boundary of areas unfit for the movement of aircraft and/ or the boundary of the aerodrome.
