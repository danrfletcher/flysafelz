PROHIBITED OR RESTRICTED AREAS
> A prohibited area is an area within which flight is entirely prohibited.

> A restricted area is an area within which some flying is restricted – for example, sometimes they only apply to certain categories of aircraft. These conditions can be found in the notes of the VFR chart and in ENR 5.1 of the AIP .

> The altitude to which they extend is stated in thousands of feet AMSL.

‘2.2’ is therefore 2,200 ft.
