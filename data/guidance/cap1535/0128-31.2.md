AERODROME LIGHT SIGNALS
The suit of light signals originate from a time when less aircraft were equipped with a radio, and ATC might routinely use light signals to control aircraft.

If you experience radio failure, look out for light signals that may be used to communicate between the ground (for example from the control tower) and aircraft.
